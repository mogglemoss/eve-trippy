# Trippy, the mascot

Trippy is the face of the bot: a retro-future robot head from a used
universe, drawn as vector and rendered at 1024×1024 for Discord. The SVG
beside this file is the source; the PNG is the export.

**Shape.** An atomic-age silhouette: a domed head with a cream racing stripe
and a teal pinline, ribbed ear grilles, a candy-striped antenna with a red
beacon, and a rivet seam. Nothing sharp anywhere near the face.

**Face.** A brass-framed CRT visor, smoked glass with scanlines and a curved
glare, and two amber phosphor eyes with a small glowing smile. The eyes are
the largest thing on the head; the smile is drawn as a lit arc so it reads
as friendly at 32 pixels.

**Finish.** Burnt-orange enamel over a cream undercoat, and the wear of a
long service: chipped paint down to the undercoat at the edges, scratches,
a rust weep under one rivet, grime toward the bottom, a stencilled "TR-01"
plate, and film grain over everything. It has been places.

**Ground.** A dark teal-navy field with a mustard badge ring and a faint
sunburst, sized so the head sits well inside Discord's circular crop.

**Palette.** Burnt orange `#e8782a`, amber `#ffc25a`, mustard `#d9a441`,
teal `#1f5b66`, red `#d4432f`, cream `#efe1bd`, ink `#15303a`. The same
colours name the embeds in `src/bot/voice.ts`.
